# Payments against a record

1. Resolve the record the user named. If the reference matches more than one, list the candidates and ask which.
2. Report the record total, how much has been applied, and what remains.
3. List each payment with its date, amount, and method.
4. Include the record's history, such as edits, credits, or write-offs, when it explains the current state.
5. Say whether the remainder is genuinely outstanding or applied but not yet cleared at the bank.
